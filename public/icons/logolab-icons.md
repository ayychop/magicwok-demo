## Appearance

- card: transparent (the source art, full bleed)
- safe-zone padding 0%, logo scale 1
- Opaque icons (apple-touch, maskable, iOS, adaptive) filled #8B1A1A (as asked).
- opaque icons padded 8%

Maskable / adaptive icons raise that padding to their own floor:
Android keeps only the centre **72dp of 108dp**, a circle of ~66% diameter,
so those files are full-bleed with the mark held inside that circle.
